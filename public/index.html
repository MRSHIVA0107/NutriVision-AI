Key Capabilities

Built for Dietary Precision & Transparency

No guessing or opaque black-box estimates. NutriVision AI merges modern multimodal vision with the authoritative scientific nutritional database.

Multimodal AI Recognition

Upload from disk or snap a photo directly using your webcam or phone camera. Deep learning extracts food candidates, categories, and confidence scores.

USDA FoodData Central

Direct connection to the USDA database containing over 900,000 reference foods. Get laboratory-tested values for vitamins, minerals, and macronutrients.

Gram-Precision Scaling

Scale nutritional values instantaneously using the standardized formula: nutrient per 100g × grams / 100 with customizable gram presets and sliders.

Interactive Chart.js Visuals

Explore dynamic macronutrient calorie splits (% Protein vs Carbs vs Fat) and compare micronutrient % Daily Values using responsive Chart.js visual charts.

Daily Meal Diary & Export

Log daily meals to a persistent browser diary, calculate running calorie and macro totals for today, and export structured JSON nutritional reports in 1 click.

Zero Exposed Secrets

Private API keys are strictly kept on the serverless backend environment, never bundled or exposed in public static client JavaScript files.

Simple 4-Step Workflow

From Food Photo to Deep Nutritional Insights

Analyze any breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack with zero friction.

1

Upload or Snap

Drag-and-drop a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or activate your webcam to take a live photo of your plate.

2

AI Identifies Dish

Backend computer vision analyzes the image, identifies food candidates, and reports confidence scores.

3

Confirm & Adjust

Confirm or select alternative candidate dishes, or use manual search. Set your portion weight in grams.

4

Instant Analysis

View your interactive Chart.js charts, FDA-format nutrition facts label, and log meals to your daily journal.
